How it works

Learn it, practise it, prepare for it — in one connected loop.

Each step feeds the next. When a skill needs more work, your child goes back to the explanation that teaches it — not a separate help page.

1

Learn

A short, structured explanation with worked examples. Read it once or return to it any time.

2

Practise

Questions by year, subject or single skill — each with a worked explanation and unlimited retries.

3

Prepare

Assessment-style sessions that mirror the format, timing and instructions of the real thing.

Illustrative example · Year 5 maths

One question, start to finish

What is 34 of 20?

5 15 ✓ 12 17

The worked explanation

  1. Find one quarter: 20 ÷ 4 = 5.
  2. Take three of them: 5 × 3 = 15.
  3. Check: 15 is three quarters of 20, and 15 < 20. ✓

The next step

Five more questions on fractions of a number — then a short check-in to make sure it stuck. See the full method.
