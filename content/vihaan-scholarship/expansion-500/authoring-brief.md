# Vihaan: 500-item scholarship draft bank

Prepare an above-average Year 5/6 learner for unofficial Huntingtower Year 7 scholarship-style practice. Retain the canonical 20-item pilot and author 480 new items. Do not copy, adapt or fetch official, commercial, textbook or website questions, passages or prompts. Use only the broad publicly described Edutest assessment areas as a format reference.

Final subject allocation: Mathematics 120; Numerical Reasoning 120; Verbal Reasoning 120; Reading Comprehension 100; Written Expression 40. Final difficulty allocation: easy 75; medium 225; challenging 200. Difficulty labels and times are editorial estimates pending human calibration.

Use the existing canonical `Question` schema, scholarship taxonomy and writing rubric. Every new item must have Australian English, original content, a meaningful skill tag, difficulty, time estimate, explanation, at least two worked steps and review guidance. MCQs need four distinct options and one defensible key. Include multi-step arithmetic, relational and conditional reasoning, varied passages and six writing modes. Writing tasks need prompt-specific planning and revision guidance, rather than a unique correct response.

Store all items as drafts with no human approver. Do not update application consumers, publication manifests, database records or taxonomy registries. Keep answer material in private authoring/review files and answer/solution fields, never visual assets, filenames, descriptive question metadata or alt text. Do not generate arbitrary SVG.

Assemble ten review units of 50. Unit 1 contains references to the retained 20 pilot items and 30 new items; units 2–10 each contain 50 new items. Independently validate each unit before writing the next, then run a consolidated validation across all 500. Block on schema, taxonomy, arithmetic consistency, duplicate options, repeated IDs, exact stems, near stems or numeric-substitution duplicates. Preserve the pilot's stricter whole-stem solvers without broadening their acceptance.

Maintain item hashes, source hashes, corpus scope, batch outcomes, coverage and outstanding review obligations. Do not call arithmetic-consistency checks a proof that a solution models its stem correctly. Human blind solving, semantic interpretation, originality review, age suitability and difficulty/time calibration remain mandatory before approval. Writing taxonomy onboarding remains a separate governed publication prerequisite.

Format reference: [Edutest's public assessment descriptions](https://www.edutest.com.au/tests_parents.html), checked 16 November 2026. No sample question material was used. New writing tasks use a 15-minute total practice window. Retained pilot writing tasks use their original 20-minute extended-practice instructions.
