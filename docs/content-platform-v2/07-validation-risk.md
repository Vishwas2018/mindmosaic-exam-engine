# Validation and Risk Model

Hard gates block progression: canonical/schema validity, answer reference integrity, deterministic solvability where supported, visual/data and asset consistency, known renderers, exact hashes/revisions, duplicate identity, curriculum/year metadata, accessibility minimums and runtime/scoring compatibility.

Risk signals escalate judgement: near duplicates/templates, originality concerns, questionable distractors, ambiguity, non-computable reasoning, novel/complex interactions, reading stimuli, Writing/rubrics, possible answer leakage and curriculum/accessibility uncertainty. Passing a heuristic does not override a hard failure.

Low-risk batch eligibility requires all hard gates, independent answer agreement/review pass, no unresolved risk, and owner QA of `max(5, ceil(10%))`, capped at batch size. Any systemic sample failure blocks the batch. High-risk items require individual owner review.
