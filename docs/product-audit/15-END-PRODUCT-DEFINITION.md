# 15. Target Product Definition

**Audit Date:** 26 August 2026  
**Auditor:** Antigravity  
**Classification:** `STRATEGIC BLUEPRINT`

---

## 1. Category A: Core Product (Must Have for Launch)

These features are non-negotiable for MindMosaic to operate as a reliable, commercial-grade Australian assessment platform:

1. **Robust Dual-Mode Assessment Engine:**
   - Untimed step-by-step practice mode with immediate worked explanations.
   - Timed exam simulation mode with candidate answer key isolation.
2. **Comprehensive Question & Visual Support:**
   - 14 supported question types (multiple choice, number entry, reading comprehension, fill blank, ordering, matching, etc.).
   - 10 deterministic structured visual renderers (charts, number lines, coordinate grids, geometry, fraction models).
   - Scratchpad digital rough paper.
3. **Persisted Student & Parent Portals:**
   - Multi-child parent dashboard with score rings, weekly minutes, and practice history.
   - Student dashboard with active session recovery, daily streak tracker, and subject mastery bars.
4. **Governed Content Depth (Minimum 3,000 Questions):**
   - Solid Grade 3 & Grade 5 NAPLAN and ICAS banks with 50+ items per cell to support 3 distinct, non-repeating mock papers per subject.
5. **Deterministic Next-Practice Recommendations:**
   - Sub-strand mistake detection (e.g. "Fractions < 60%") with a 1-click 5-question reinforcement launcher.
6. **Stripe Billing & Entitlements:**
   - Self-serve Family Monthly ($19/mo) and Family Annual ($180/yr) checkout, customer portal, and 14-day trial management.

---

## 2. Category B: Premium Product (Should Have)

Capabilities that elevate MindMosaic into an indispensable household education tool:

1. **Printable Diagnostic Reports (PDF):**
   - High-fidelity downloadable PDF summary of child strengths, gap analysis, and NAPLAN band projection for parent-teacher interviews.
2. **Weekly Parent Progress Email Digest:**
   - Automated Sunday evening summary email showing weekly practice time, top improvements, and recommended focus for the coming week.
3. **Student First-Run Orientation Diagnostic:**
   - 5-question quick warmup upon account creation to establish initial baseline ability.
4. **Spaced Repetition "Mistake Bank":**
   - A dedicated personal revision deck where questions answered incorrectly reappear 3 days and 7 days later.

---

## 3. Category C: Expansion Capabilities (Post-Launch Horizons)

Expansion streams once Core V1 is established:

1. **Selective School & Scholarship Entry (NSW / VIC):**
   - Grade 6 / Year 7 Thinking Skills, Mathematical Reasoning, and Advanced Reading comprehension.
2. **Mathematics Competitions & Olympiads:**
   - Australian Mathematics Competition (AMC) Middle/Upper Primary papers.
   - Singapore Maths bar-modelling heuristic problem sets.
3. **Audio-Assisted Spelling:**
   - Audio dictation pronunciation player for Spelling tests.

---

## 4. Category D: Future Intelligence (Deferred to Wave 4+)

1. **Adaptive Multistage Testing (MST / IRT):**
   - Dynamic routing of testlets based on real-time psychometric Rasch / 2PL ability estimation ($\theta$).
2. **Empirical Difficulty & Distractor Calibration:**
   - Automatically adjusting item difficulty based on aggregate student response logs.

---

## 5. Category E: Do NOT Build Yet (Explicitly Deferred)

Avoid unnecessary enterprise or consumer complexity:

* ❌ **Gamified Virtual Currencies & Avatar Stores:** Distracts primary learners from genuine academic achievement.
* ❌ **Public Social Leaderboards:** Induces unhealthy anxiety among young children; progress should be individual and encouraging.
* ❌ **Unmoderated AI Essay Auto-Grading:** High risk of hallucinated feedback and compliance issues on primary school writing.
* ❌ **Enterprise School SSO (SAML/SCIM):** Unnecessary for a direct-to-consumer parent/family platform.
* ❌ **Real-Time Multiplayer Kahoot-Style Games:** High infrastructure cost with minimal diagnostic learning value.
