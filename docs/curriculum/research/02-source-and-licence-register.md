## 1. Executive Summary & Legal Guardrails

MindMosaic is a commercial online education platform. Ensuring rigorous intellectual property compliance and respecting statutory copyright across national, state, and territory curriculum authorities is a non-negotiable legal requirement.

### Conservative Legal Principles:
1. **Original Content Primacy:** All practice questions, explanations, and learning activities must be 100% originally authored from first principles.
2. **No Blanket Assumptions on Non-Copyrightability:** While individual alphanumeric codes or single short titles may be factual references, entire curriculum taxonomies, structured selections, elaborations, and descriptive prose constitute copyright-protected works under the *Copyright Act 1968 (Cth)*.
3. **No 'Store-Only' Safe Harbor:** Storing unlicensed copyrighted material in private databases or backend stores does not make reproduction lawful without a valid licence or statutory exception.
4. **Commercial Product Status:** MindMosaic is a commercial SaaS product. Licences containing a Non-Commercial condition (such as VCAA's **CC BY-NC 3.0 AU**) do **not** permit the reproduction of official curriculum text bodies within commercial subscriber views.
5. **Legally Defensive Access Statuses:**
   - `display_permitted`: Explicitly permitted under an open commercial-use licence (e.g. ACARA CC BY 4.0 with attribution).
   - `metadata_reference_only`: Permissible short citation of official code, node title, and official hyperlink for identification purposes only.
   - `permission_required`: Direct reproduction of extended text requires express written commercial agreement from the authority.
   - `legal_review_required`: Use requires formal corporate legal review before publication.

## 3. Data-Access Modalities: Facts vs Inferences

MindMosaic strictly distinguishes between proven public data feeds and authenticated/unsupported tools:

1. **Public Bulk Machine-Readable Endpoints:**
   - **ACARA MRAC:** Publicly accessible SPARQL endpoint (`https://rdf.australiancurriculum.edu.au/api/sparql`) and bulk downloadable JSON-LD / RDF / XML / CSV packages under CC BY 4.0.
2. **Public HTML Web Portals (No Bulk API):**
   - **VCAA:** Content must be indexed via public web pages (`https://f10.vcaa.vic.edu.au/`) or static downloadable PDF/Word tables. *There is no public REST API.*
   - **NESA:** Digital syllabus pages and downloadable CSV tables via `https://curriculum.nsw.edu.au/`. *No public unauthenticated API exists.*
   - **SCSA:** KΓÇô10 Outline web pages and syllabus tables via `https://k10outline.scsa.wa.edu.au/`.
3. **Authenticated / Restricted Teacher Tools:**
   - **QCAA PΓÇô10 Planning App:** Available exclusively to registered Queensland teachers with state educational credentials; **not** a public data pipeline.

## 4. IP Implementation Strategy for MindMosaic

1. **Code & Title Citation (`metadata_reference_only`):** MindMosaic indexes official curriculum codes (`VC2M3N01`, `MA2-RN-01`, `AC9M3N01`) and standard topic titles solely as taxonomy pointers.
2. **Proprietary Parent Summaries:** All descriptive explanations, conceptual breakdowns ("What this means in plain English"), and family activities ("Try this at home") are 100% originally authored by MindMosaic, creating independent copyright assets owned by MindMosaic.
3. **Transparent Outbound Linking:** When a parent or educator wishes to view the full official statutory text, MindMosaic provides an outbound hyperlink directly to the official government source page (`rel="noopener noreferrer"`).
