## 1. Governance context

- **Phase 1 (PR #74):** a truthful fail-closed representation, `kind: legacy_import`, with original generation `unverified`. Phase 1 gates refuse legacy-import content and are unchanged.
- **ADR-017** (`docs/adr/017-legacy-content-unknown-origin-eligibility.md`), adopted as **policy only** on 2026-10-24. It authorises design work for a narrow deterministic admission route. It authorises no code, no gate change and no admission. Implementation, admission and release each need a separate owner approval.
- **Review independence policy** (`docs/REVIEW_INDEPENDENCE_POLICY.md`): judgement content generated by an AI needs a different-provider reviewer. That rule can only be evaluated when the generator identity is known. For unknown-origin content, independent review is `not_established_unknown_origin`, permanently, and null or `other/unknown` identities cannot satisfy either independence rule.

| **Independent reviewer identity and qualification** | `not_established` | No qualified human reviewer has been engaged. Model-generated reviews on file are advisory and carry no reviewer qualification |

A Git author, a `Co-Authored-By` trailer, an importer, a historical declaration or a reviewing model is **not proof** of the original generator. Earlier packages said "all 160 originated from Anthropic Claude" in one place and "all 160 unverified" in another. The consistent position is: Claude is the generator the import workflow *declared*; it is not a verified fact, and no field may be populated to suggest otherwise.

**What this means for review.** Because the generator is unknown, a new ChatGPT, Gemini or Qwen review does not satisfy the different-provider rule, and it cannot be described as production-grade independent review. It may be collected as **advisory evidence**, subject to a separate policy decision on how, if at all, such evidence counts.
