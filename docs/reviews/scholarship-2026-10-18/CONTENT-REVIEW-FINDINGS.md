# Scholarship Year 6: review of all 160 questions (18 Oct 2026)

Author of this review: Claude Sonnet 5.5 in Claude Code. The drafts were generated by Claude, so this is an advisory review by the same provider family. It is **not** independent evidence for any gate and **not** a human approval. Nothing here is approved, signed, published or enabled.

## How the review was done

1. Every one of the 160 questions was solved **blind**: prompt, passage and options first, key and explanation unread, working written out, answer chosen. Only afterwards were the answers compared with the keys.
2. **Result: 160 of 160 independent answers match the declared key.** (Mathematics 40, Numerical Reasoning 40, Verbal Reasoning 45, Reading 35.) That is a check of key correctness, not of editorial quality.
3. Separately, the first-party recompute proves 60 keys (all 40 mathematics, NR 21 to 40), and the closed-language solvers prove the remaining NR sequences and grids plus the letter, code and syllogism items. Reading and the vocabulary and analogy items have no machine proof; for those the only evidence is human or different-provider judgement.
4. The same drafts were then read for the criteria in the brief: single defensible answer, plausible and distinct distractors, explanation quality, Australian English, year-level fit, passage consistency, accessibility and originality. Findings follow.

Australian English: a search for common US spellings found none. Accessibility: no item uses an image or a visual, so there is no alt text to leak an answer. Answer positions are balanced in every section.

## Other defects found and fixed

- `vr-025` said "a marked field where football is played"; in Australian English that is ambiguous across codes. Now "where soccer is played" (and the explanation).
- **Key identifiable by length (Reading).** In 18 of 35 Reading items the correct option was the single longest, and in 8 it was 1.5 to 2 times the average distractor (rc-005, 009, 018, 022, 029, 030, 031, 034). Distractors were lengthened to match, and the keys of rc-009, 029, 030 and 034 shortened. The key is now the single longest option in 14 of 35 items (was 18), and no key is more than 1.45 times the average distractor (was 2.0).
- The reading pattern could not draw a paper at all (its source filtered `multiple_choice` but the items are `reading_comprehension`) and had no whole-passage rule. Both fixed; covered by tests.

## Originality

The automated originality gate passed all 80 NR and mathematics candidates, and no item reproduces text from NAPLAN, ICAS, textbooks or commercial papers that I know of. That is a check against the factory's own corpus only. Several mathematics and reasoning items are classic structures (heads and legs, ages, consecutive numbers, letter series); the wording and numbers are original, but originality is Vish's tick to give, not mine.
