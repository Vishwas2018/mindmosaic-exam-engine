### 1.4 vish-sp-m7-math-004 — Compound percentage change ⚠️ CRITICAL ISSUE

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Subject** | Mathematics |
| **Strand / Topic** | Number / Percentages |
| **Difficulty** | Medium |
| **Marks** | 2 |
| **Estimated time** | 90 seconds |
| **Question type** | Multiple choice |

**Question stem:**
> A shirt costs $40. Its price increases by 25% in January, then decreases by 20% in February. What is the final price?

**Answer options:**
- a) $50
- b) $45
- c) $55
- d) $60

**Correct answer:** a) $50

**Explanation:** 25% of $40 = $10. New price = $40 + $10 = $50.

**Review finding:** ❌ **CRITICAL — Question is broken.**

**Defects identified:**
1. **Correct answer not among options.** The compound calculation is: January: $40 × 1.25 = $50; February: $50 × 0.80 = $40. The final price is $40, which is not listed in any option.
2. **Answer key is wrong.** The key points to option (a) $50, which is the price after January only, not after both changes.
3. **Explanation is incomplete.** It only accounts for the January increase, ignoring the February decrease entirely.
4. **Difficulty mismatch.** Stem title says "Compound percentage change" but options and explanation treat it as a single-step change.

**Required fix (before human approval):**
- Correct the final price calculation to $40 and add it as an option
- Fix the answer key to point to the correct option
- Rewrite the explanation to show both steps

### 2.2 vish-sp-m7-nr-002 — Analogous number pair ⚠️ CRITICAL ISSUE

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Subject** | Numerical Reasoning |
| **Strand / Topic** | Relationships / Proportional reasoning |
| **Difficulty** | Medium |
| **Marks** | 2 |
| **Estimated time** | 90 seconds |
| **Question type** | Multiple choice |

**Question stem:**
> 2 is to 10 as 7 is to __?

**Answer options:**
- a) 45
- b) 30
- c) 60
- d) 75

**Correct answer:** c) 60

**Explanation:** The relationship is n * 5: 2 * 5 = 10. For 7: 7 * 5 = 35... actually n^2 + 1 then * 2: 2^2+1=5, 5*2=10. For 7: 7^2+1=50.

**Review finding:** ❌ **CRITICAL — Question is broken.**

**Defects identified:**
1. **Correct answer not among options.** If the relationship is n × 5, then 7 × 5 = 35, which is not an option. If the relationship is n² + 1 then × 2, then (7² + 1) × 2 = 100, which is not an option. Option (c) 60 does not match any coherent relationship.
2. **Answer key is wrong.** Points to option (c) 60 with no supporting calculation.
3. **Explanation is contradictory.** The explanation starts with "n * 5" (which gives 35), then switches to "n² + 1 then * 2" (which gives 100), neither matching option (c) 60.
4. **Stem / options mismatch.** Stem was changed to "2 is to 10 as 7 is to __?" but options were not updated from the original "12 is to 36 as 15 is to __?" set.

**Required fix (before human approval):**
- Choose one coherent relationship and update both stem and options consistently
- Fix the answer key to point to the correct option
- Rewrite the explanation to match the chosen relationship
