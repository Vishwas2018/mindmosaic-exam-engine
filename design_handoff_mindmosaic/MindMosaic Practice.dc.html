Question 4 · Multiple choice Skill: Equivalence and comparison

Which fraction is closest to one half?

{{ verdict }}

Why 5/8 is closest

One half is 4/8. The distance from 5/8 to 4/8 is 1/8. Compare that with 2/3, which sits 1/6 above one half, and with 1/3, which sits 1/6 below. Converting every option to a common denominator makes the comparison direct rather than a guess.
