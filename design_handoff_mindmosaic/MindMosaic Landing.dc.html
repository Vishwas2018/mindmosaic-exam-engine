Not correct — let’s look at it together

You answered 3/4. The correct answer is 5/8.

Worked explanation

  1. Count the equal spaces between 0 and 1. There are eight, so each step is 1/8.
  2. Count the steps from 0 to the arrow. The arrow sits on the fifth mark.
  3. Five steps of 1/8 gives 5/8.

A common slip is counting the marks instead of the spaces.

Quality and originality

Purpose-built questions, reviewed against seven standards.

MindMosaic questions are purpose-built and reviewed for correctness, clarity, age suitability, visual consistency, Australian English, accessibility and originality.

  1. 01

    Correctness

    The stated answer is checked before the question is published.

  2. 02

    Clarity

    One defensible answer, and no trick phrasing.

  3. 03

    Age suitability

    Reading load reviewed against the year level, not just the topic.

  4. 04

    Visual consistency

    Diagrams drawn to one house style and one scale system.

  5. 05

    Australian English

    Spelling, currency, metric units and local settings throughout.

  6. 06

    Accessibility

    Text alternatives, keyboard paths and contrast reviewed on every item.

  7. 07

    Originality

    Reviewed against published material. Nothing is reproduced.

  8. 08

    Explanation Quality

    Every item ships with a worked explanation a child can read alone.

  9. 09

    Difficulty Calibration

    Trialled with children at the year level before it is released.

  10. 09

    Annual Review

    Items are revisited frequently and retired if they stop working

Questions

Answers before you sign up.

Still unsure about something? Get help and a person will reply.

Which year levels are supported?+

The platform is built for Years 1 to 12, and each programme lists the year levels it currently covers. Where coverage is still being confirmed, the programme says so rather than implying availability.

Which learning programmes are available?+

Structured Australian Curriculum learning pathways, Singapore Maths, and the Learning Hub library of explanations, worked examples and skill lessons. Exam preparation covers NAPLAN-style, ICAS-style, AMC-style and selective school entry-style formats.

What is the difference between learning, practice and exam simulation?+

Learning teaches the concept with explanations and worked examples. Practice is flexible: submit an answer and the worked explanation follows, with retry and review available. An exam simulation runs under assessment conditions — realistic instructions, section navigation, flag for review, autosave and a review-before-submit screen — with results and explanations released after submission.

What does “assessment-style” mean?+

It describes the format, question types and conditions of an assessment — not the assessment itself. MindMosaic is an independent learning platform. Its assessment-style materials contain original questions and are not official examinations, past papers or endorsed preparation materials. Assessment Disclaimer

How is my child’s data handled?+

A student account is created by a parent and holds a first name and year level. We do not sell personal information and there is no third-party advertising. Full details of storage, retention and deletion are set out in the Privacy Policy.

Need more detail? See the Help Centre, Privacy Policy, Terms and Conditions and Assessment Disclaimer.
